Quantum Tunnel Detection System

System Overview

This system simulates atom interferometry for underground structure detection. Traditional sensors struggle with Quantum Shot Noise—random fluctuations that obscure the gravity signal. The Dice Coefficient (also called the F1 Score) measures overlap between the AI's tunnel prediction and the actual tunnel (Ground Truth).

  • ● Perfect Alignment (1.0 / 100%): The AI's prediction and the real tunnel overlap perfectly.
  • ● Zero Agreement (0.0 / 0%): The AI missed the tunnel or hallucinated a structure in the wrong location.
  • ● Why not "Accuracy"? In gravity mapping, 95% is solid rock. A model guessing "nothing" gets 95% accuracy. Dice forces precision on actual tunnel pixels.
Baseline (Traditional Math)

Z-score thresholding on the raw sensor data cannot separate noise from signal. In high-noise regimes, accuracy collapses to near zero.

U-Net (Quantum AI)

Deep learning trained on quantum noise physics. It denoises the sensor readout in real time and reconstructs hidden tunnel geometry with high fidelity.
